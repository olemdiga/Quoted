Loneliness vs Solitude

One is poverty of self. The other is richness of self. Know which you are in.

Loneliness is the poverty of self. Solitude is the richness of self.

Loneliness is the pain of being alone. Solitude is the glory of being alone.

When you are lonely, you are looking for someone to fill a void. When you are in solitude, you are filling yourself.

Loneliness is a hunger signal. Solitude is a feast.

The lonely person wants to be with others but cannot. The solitary person can be with others but chooses not to.

Loneliness is the feeling that something is missing. Solitude is the feeling that nothing is missing.

In loneliness, you are running from yourself. In solitude, you are running toward yourself.

Solitude is the laboratory of the soul. Loneliness is the prison of the self.

The lonely person seeks company to escape their own thoughts. The solitary person seeks aloneness to encounter them.

Loneliness is a wound. Solitude is the healing.

You can be lonely in a crowd. You can be in solitude on a mountain. The difference is not the number of people but the quality of your inner life.

Loneliness is the absence of connection. Solitude is the presence of self-connection.

The cure for loneliness is not other people. It is a self you can bear to be alone with.

Solitude is not about being alone. It is about being enough for yourself.

Loneliness is the shadow cast by a self you have not yet met.

In solitude, the mind becomes a cathedral. In loneliness, it becomes a prison cell.

Solitude is not the absence of others. It is the presence of oneself.

Loneliness asks: why am I alone? Solitude answers: because I am enough.

The capacity for solitude is the measure of your relationship with yourself.

Loneliness is the tax you pay for not knowing who you are. Solitude is the dividend of self-knowledge.

You cannot escape loneliness by adding people. You can only transform it into solitude by deepening yourself.

The lonely seek to be seen. The solitary see themselves.

Solitude is the garden where the self grows. Loneliness is the desert where it withers.

Loneliness is a state of lack. Solitude is a state of fullness.

The greatest journeys are taken in solitude. The greatest prisons are built in loneliness.
