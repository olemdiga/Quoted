Will to Power

The fundamental drive to overcome, create, and assert one's own values upon the world

The will to power is not a desire for domination over others. It is the drive to overcome oneself, to become who you are.

What is good? All that heightens the feeling of power, the will to power, power itself in man.

The will to power is the fundamental force of life - not survival, not reproduction, but the drive to expand, to overcome resistance, to grow.

This world is the will to power - and nothing besides. And you yourselves are also this will to power - and nothing besides.

To will is to command. The will to power is the capacity to set your own values and live by them, regardless of what the herd believes.

The will to power is not about having power over others. It is about having power over yourself - self-mastery, self-overcoming, self-creation.

Every living thing does everything it can not to preserve itself but to become more.

The will to power is the instinct for freedom. It is the drive to shape your environment rather than be shaped by it.

What man wants, what every smallest part of a living organism wants, is an increase of power.

The will to power is expressed not in taking from others, but in creating value that others recognize and respect.

To those who have the will to power, resistance is not an obstacle. It is the necessary condition for growth.

The weak resent the strong because they lack the will to power. The strong do not resent; they overcome.

The will to power is the answer to the question: why do anything at all? Because to act, to create, to overcome is to be alive.

You do not discover your values. You create them. That is the will to power in its highest form.

The will to power is the force that turns suffering into meaning, obstacles into opportunities, and chaos into order.

Every great achievement is an expression of the will to power - the refusal to accept limitations, the insistence on imposing one's vision on reality.

The will to power is not loud. It is the quiet discipline of daily self-overcoming.

To live is to will. And to will is to will power - the capacity to act, to change, to become.

The highest expression of the will to power is not conquest but creation - the ability to bring something new into the world.

The will to power is the sovereign's relationship to life. The slave waits for permission. The sovereign acts.

What does not kill me makes me stronger - but only if I will it so. Suffering without the will to power is just suffering.

The will to power is the antidote to nihilism. When you create your own values, the void becomes a canvas.

You are not a passive recipient of life. You are a force. The will to power is the recognition of this truth.

The will to power is the courage to say: I will live by my own law, and I will bear the consequences.
