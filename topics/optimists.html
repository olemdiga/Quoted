Optimists

Those who see opportunity in every difficulty rather than difficulty in every opportunity

The optimist sees the donut, the pessimist sees the hole.

Optimists are focused on rewards. Pessimists are focused on risks.

An optimist expects his dreams to come true; a pessimist expects his nightmares to.

I'm always looking for a way to make it happen, not excuses why it won't work.

You'll never meet a very successful pessimistic person.

Both optimists and pessimists contribute to society. The optimist invents the aeroplane, the pessimist the parachute.

Inside every cynical person, there is a disappointed idealist.

Optimism is the faith that leads to achievement. Nothing can be done without hope and confidence.

A pessimist sees the difficulty in every opportunity; an optimist sees the opportunity in every difficulty.

Keep your face always toward the sunshine - and shadows will fall behind you.

Optimism is a strategy for making a better future. Unless you believe the future can be better, you won't step up to make it so.

Optimists don't ignore problems - they just believe they can solve them.

Pessimism leads to weakness, optimism to power.

Optimism is not about ignoring reality; it's about focusing on the part of reality that contains solutions.

Optimism is a form of mental courage that believes in the light even when all around is dark.

Optimism is the one quality more associated with success and happiness than any other.

The optimist invents the future by believing in its possibility today.

Optimism is the engine of achievement. Without it, even the most talented remain parked.

Optimism is the foundation of courage, and true courage is the foundation of all achievement.
