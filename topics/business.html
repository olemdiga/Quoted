Business

The architecture of value creation and sustainable enterprise

A business that cannot survive without you is not a business - it is a job with extra risk.

When you are small, there is no excuse for poor customer service. It is the most reliable touchpoint to keep selling your service.

Profit sustains mission; ethics sustains legacy. One without the other is a countdown to irrelevance.

A business grows through systems, not heroics. If you are the only one who can solve every problem, you do not have a business. You have a hostage situation.

Price is what you pay. Value is what you deliver. Confuse the two at your peril.

Hiring the right people is the toughest part of running a business. A single wrong hire can poison the well for everyone else.

It is better to let people learn from their failures than to always do the hard things for them. Rescue creates dependency. Recovery creates competence.

A team's job is to collaborate and find solutions together, not to blindly follow orders and wait for clues when something goes contrary to plan.

Marketing is the key. Even if your product is the greatest invention since the wheel, people who have not heard about it simply will not buy it.

Perfection is rarely reached in a business environment, but that should not allow for sloppiness. The gap between perfect and sloppy is where reputation lives.

Customers help improve the business. Listen to them closely. They will tell you what to build next.

Be independent from your family but loyal at the same time. Keep your friends for friendship but work with the skilled and competent.

Resist the temptation of working with family and friends. Peers are peers, not boss and employee. The power differential corrupts the relationship.

You will waste your life trying to make those around you into who you want them to be. Hire the person, not the potential.

The best business strategy is one that creates value for customers while building moats against competitors.

Cash flow is the oxygen of business. Without it, even the most brilliant ideas suffocate.

Scale reveals what character built. What works for ten customers often breaks for ten thousand.

The market does not care about your effort. It only cares about your results. Effort is for the gym. Outcomes are for business.

Innovation happens at the intersection of customer needs and emerging possibilities. Listen to the first. Watch the second.

A sustainable business solves a real problem for real people at a price they are willing to pay.

The purpose of a business is to create and keep a customer. Everything else is commentary.

Culture eats strategy for breakfast. You can have the perfect plan, but if your people do not trust each other, the plan will fail.

Do not confuse motion with progress. A rocking horse moves but goes nowhere.

The entrepreneur's greatest skill is not vision. It is the ability to hear a thousand nos and still believe the next door might open.
