Minimum Viable Day

The recovery mode definition. On hard days, the bar is low enough to step over.

On the hard days, the bar is: Out of bed. Two meals. One hour of focus. Ten minutes outside. Sleep. Meeting the minimum is a complete victory.

The minimum viable day is not a failure mode. It is a survival protocol. It keeps the system running when full performance is impossible.

Lower the bar until it is absurd. Then step over it. You are not a machine. You are a creature with fluctuating capacity.

Anything beyond the minimum is DLC. You are not required to complete the expansion pack today.

The minimum viable day is the emergency room of productivity. It keeps you alive so you can fight another day.

Define success so narrowly that it becomes impossible to fail. That is the art of the minimum viable day.

When your bandwidth is narrow, narrow your expectations. The day is not lost if you do one thing.

The minimum viable day is not about laziness. It is about honoring your actual capacity instead of berating yourself for your imagined capacity.

On recovery days, the goal is not progress. The goal is maintenance. Keep the pilot light on.

The minimum viable day is a contract with yourself that you cannot break because the terms are too generous.

Some days you build the cathedral. Some days you just make sure the scaffolding doesn't fall down. Both are essential.

The minimum viable day prevents the downward spiral. One bad day does not become a bad week if you hit the minimum.

Perfectionism demands a cathedral every day. The minimum viable day asks only for a single brick.

Your capacity is not a constant. It is a variable. The minimum viable day is the equation that works across the entire range.

The wisdom of the minimum viable day is knowing that showing up at 10% is infinitely better than showing up at 0%.

Define your minimum before the hard day arrives. When the fog descends, you will not have the clarity to negotiate with yourself.

The minimum viable day is the grace you extend to your future self, written in advance.

Meeting the minimum is not settling. It is winning the battle against the part of you that wants to do nothing.

The minimum viable day is the safety net that allows you to walk the tightrope of ambition without falling into the abyss of burnout.

You are allowed to have off days. The minimum viable day is the protocol that ensures off days don't become lost weeks.

The minimum viable day is the difference between resting and quitting. One is recovery. The other is abandonment.

On the days you cannot run, walk. On the days you cannot walk, crawl. But always move toward the direction you have chosen.

The minimum viable day is the embodiment of self-compassion in the language of systems.
