The Narcissist Filter

How to spot them before they spot you

Watch how someone treats people who can do nothing for them. That's the narcissist filter in its purest form.

Narcissists reveal themselves in how they respond to the word "no." A healthy person negotiates; a narcissist retaliates.

The fastest narcissist filter: set a small boundary early. Their reaction tells you everything.

Narcissists are allergic to accountability. Ask about a mistake they made and watch the deflection begin.

Listen to how someone talks about their exes. If every story casts them as the innocent victim, run.

The narcissist filter activates when you achieve something. Do they celebrate you or immediately compare?

Pay attention to who dominates conversations. Narcissists don't converse; they perform monologues with occasional pauses for applause.

Narcissists love bomb early and intensely. If it feels too good to be true, your filter should be screaming.

The narcissist filter is simple: observe how they treat service workers. Character is revealed in interactions without status.

Narcissists cannot genuinely apologize. Listen for "I'm sorry you feel that way" - that's not an apology; that's a dismissal.

A narcissist's empathy is performative. Watch for the gap between their words and their follow-through.

The narcissist filter catches those who need to be the smartest person in every room - and will diminish others to stay there.

Narcissists recruit allies before conflict. Notice who triangulates rather than addresses issues directly.

The narcissist's grandiosity is a filter all by itself. Claims of extraordinary victimhood or exceptionalism are red flags.

Observe how someone reacts when you share good news. Narcissists either one-up you or diminish your achievement.

Narcissists have shallow relationship histories. Look for a pattern of short-term connections and dramatic endings.

The narcissist filter catches those who cannot tolerate being teased. Fragile egos can't handle playful critique.

Narcissists confuse envy with admiration. They believe others want to be them, not that others might simply be happy.

The narcissist filter is most accurate when you're leaving. How someone handles endings reveals their true character.

Narcissists out themselves through their relationship with truth. Exaggerations, omissions, and convenient narratives are their currency.

Trust your body's filter. That feeling of being drained after interactions is your nervous system recognizing danger.

Narcissists collect people like objects. Notice who treats relationships as acquisitions rather than connections.

The narcissist filter catches those who cannot celebrate others' wins without inserting themselves into the story.

Pay attention to who makes everything about them. Even your pain becomes their opportunity for attention.

Develop your narcissist filter early. The cost of missing the signs is paid in years of confusion and recovery.
