Attention

The finite resource that defines your reality

If you don't like something, take away its only power: your attention.

What you pay attention to grows. What you ignore withers. This is not mysticism. This is the architecture of cognition.

Your attention is not infinite. It is a daily allowance that does not roll over. Spend it on what you intend to build.

In the attention economy, you are not the customer. You are the product. The transaction is your focus exchanged for their revenue.

The ability to direct your attention deliberately, rather than having it hijacked by the loudest stimulus, is the foundation of sovereignty.

Where attention goes, energy flows. Where energy flows, results grow. The equation is simple. The execution is not.

Attention is the rarest form of generosity because it cannot be faked. You either give it or you don't.

Multitasking is the art of paying attention to nothing while convincing yourself you're paying attention to everything.

The mind is its own place. It can make a heaven of hell or a hell of heaven. The difference is where you place your attention.

Attention is the beginning of devotion. What you attend to, you serve. What you serve, you become.

The faculty of bringing back a wandering attention, over and over again, is the very root of judgment, character, and will.

Your attention span is your lifespan measured in focus. How you spend it determines what you become.

The real power is not in having access to information. It is in controlling what information gets access to you.

When you give someone your full attention, you give them a piece of your life you will never recover. Choose wisely.

Distraction is not the enemy of focus. It is the default state. Focus is the anomaly that must be deliberately cultivated.

You cannot think your way into better attention. You can only act your way there. The body leads. The mind follows.

Notifications are not neutral. They are bids for your attention placed by algorithms optimized for engagement, not for your flourishing.

The quality of your attention determines the quality of your experience. There is no other variable.

Boredom is not a signal to seek stimulation. It is a signal that your attention is unmoored and seeking a worthy anchor.

To protect your attention is to protect your life. The two are not separable.
