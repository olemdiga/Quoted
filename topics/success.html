Success

The intersection of preparation, opportunity, and relentless execution

All self-help boils down to choose long-term over short-term.

The most successful people I know believe in themselves almost to the point of delusion.

There is no formula for success, but there are two formulas for failure: not trying and not persisting.

Success doesn't come to you. You go to it.

Success is peace of mind which is a direct result of self-satisfaction in knowing you did your best to become the best you are capable of becoming.

A man is a success if he gets up in the morning and gets to bed at night, and in between he does what he wants to do.

Most overnight successes take at least 5 years. Budget your life accordingly.

Success is a lousy teacher. It seduces smart people into thinking they can't lose.

Success is the ability to go from one failure to another with no loss of enthusiasm.

Success lies in relentless execution of the basics.

The way to succeed is to double your failure rate.

Doubt kills more dreams than failure ever will.

Success is not the key to happiness. Happiness is the key to success. If you love what you are doing, you will be successful.

The successful warrior is the average man, with laser-like focus.

Success usually comes to those who are too busy to be looking for it.

Don't aim for success if you want it; just do what you love and believe in, and it will come naturally.

Success is stumbling from failure to failure with no loss of enthusiasm.

The price of success is hard work, dedication to the job at hand, and the determination that whether we win or lose, we have applied the best of ourselves to the task at hand.

Success is not in what you have, but who you have become.

Success is getting what you want. Happiness is wanting what you get.

The road to success and the road to failure are almost exactly the same.

Success is the sum of small efforts, repeated day in and day out.

Success is not just about making money. It's about making a difference.

Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts.

The only place where success comes before work is in the dictionary.

Success is the progressive realization of a worthy goal or ideal.

Success is the maximum utilization of the ability that you have.

Success is not measured by what you accomplish, but by the opposition you have encountered, and the courage with which you have maintained the struggle against overwhelming odds.

Success is the quality of the journey, not the destination.

Success is the ability to live your life in your own way, to be the person you want to be, to do what you want to do.
