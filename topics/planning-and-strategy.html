Planning & Strategy

Plans are useless, but planning is indispensable. The act prepares the mind for the unexpected.

Plans are like scaffolding. They enable construction but must be removed to reveal the building.

The value of planning is not in the plan but in the act of planning. It prepares the mind.

Strategy is the art of trading the unnecessary for the essential.

A plan is a commitment to a future that will not exist. Hold it lightly.

Over-planning is the art of building a bridge before you know where the river is.

The best strategy is one that adapts faster than the enemy adapts.

Planning without action is a fantasy. Action without planning is a gamble.

A strategy is a hypothesis about the future. Test it, refine it, abandon it if needed.

The general who plans for everything plans for nothing. Focus is the essence of strategy.

Plans are maps drawn in pencil. The terrain will demand erasures.

Strategy is the allocation of scarce resources to achieve asymmetric advantage.

The difference between a plan and a strategy: a plan tells you what to do; a strategy tells you what not to do.

The purpose of planning is not to eliminate uncertainty but to navigate it with grace.

A good plan executed today is better than a perfect plan executed never.

Strategy is the art of sacrifice. You cannot win everywhere.

Plans are the scaffolding of achievement. But do not mistake the scaffolding for the cathedral.

The best planners are those who plan for plans to fail.

Strategy is the discipline of saying no to good opportunities so you can say yes to great ones.

The map is not the journey, but a journey without a map is a wandering.

Plans are commitments. Strategy is adaptation. Both are necessary, but only one survives contact with reality.

The essence of strategy is choosing what not to do. It is the art of exclusion.

A plan is a story we tell ourselves about the future. The future rarely listens to stories.
