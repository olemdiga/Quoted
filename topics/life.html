Life

The brief, beautiful, bewildering journey between two mysteries

What's life? Showing up for the people you love, again and again, until you can't show up anymore.

Life is what happens to you while you're busy making other plans.

The purpose of life is not to be happy. It is to be useful, to be honorable, to be compassionate, to have it make some difference that you have lived and lived well.

Life is not a problem to be solved, but a reality to be experienced.

Life is the art of drawing without an eraser.

Life is a shipwreck, but we must not forget to sing in the lifeboats.

The meaning of life is to find your gift. The purpose of life is to give it away.

Life is a beautiful struggle between becoming who you are and remembering who you've always been.

Life is too important to be taken seriously.

Life is a series of thousands of tiny miracles. Notice them.

Life is a journey that must be traveled no matter how bad the roads and accommodations.

Life is either a daring adventure or nothing at all.

The great tragedy of life is not that we die, but that we stop truly living long before death arrives.

Life is a balance between holding on and letting go.

Life is a mirror and will reflect back to the thinker what he thinks into it.

Life is about using the whole box of crayons.

Life is a succession of lessons which must be lived to be understood.

Life is a flower of which love is the honey.

Life is a puzzle - you have to find the pieces that fit and accept the ones that don't.

Life is a wave - you can't avoid the crashes, but you can learn to surf.

The secret of life is to fall seven times and get up eight.

Life is a book, and those who do not travel read only one page.

Life is a canvas - throw all the paint on it you can.

Life is a question and how we live it is our answer.

Life is a beautiful melody, only the lyrics are messed up.

Life is a classroom - you're given the lesson first and the test afterward.

Life is a gift, and it offers us the privilege, opportunity, and responsibility to give something back.

Life is a dance between making it happen and letting it happen.

Life is a journey that has its trials, its obstacles, and its difficulties - but also its great rewards.

Life is a beautiful, terrible, perfect mess - and that's what makes it worth living.
