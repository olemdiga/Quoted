Substances

The chemistry of consciousness and the cost of escape

The dose makes the poison. The intention makes the medicine. Context transforms a substance from curse to cure.

Drink alcohol only if it tastes good, and never to get drunk.

When drinking, consume at least as much water as alcohol. A glass of water before and after each drink.

There is a thin line between medicines, supplements, and drugs. Whether they are good or bad depends mostly on dosage and intention.

Experimenting with cognition enhancement should not be a crime, as long as it is safe.

People struggling with addiction need help, not punishment.

It is strange that drugs are persecuted while alcohol is glorified to the point that a non-drinker is often stigmatized.

Clear thinking is powerful. Do not cloud it with substances unless it is rare and intentional.

Addiction is often a symptom of deeper pain. Treat the wound, not just the behavior.

Mind-altering substances should expand consciousness, not escape it. Use them as tools for exploration, not crutches for avoidance.

Cultural hypocrisy around substances reveals more about social control than about actual harm reduction.

The most dangerous addiction is to the idea that you need something external to be complete.

Substances do not create new realities. They merely alter your perception of the one that already exists.

True freedom is the ability to enjoy substances without being controlled by them.

Alcohol is borrowing happiness from tomorrow. The interest rates are brutal.

Recovery is not about abstinence. It is about rebuilding a life so fulfilling that escape loses its appeal.

Substance use becomes abuse when it becomes the primary way you cope with reality.

The wisest approach to any substance is respect for its power and awareness of your own vulnerability.

Altered states should complement ordinary consciousness, not replace it. Visit other realms, but remember where home is.

The goal is not prohibition but education. Understand how substances affect mind, body, and society.
