Gratitude

The art of finding abundance in what already exists

A good life is not a life without problems. A good life is a life with good problems.

Dead people receive more flowers than the living ones because regret is stronger than gratitude.

How lucky I am to have something that makes saying goodbye so hard.

Feeling gratitude and not expressing it is like wrapping a present and not giving it.

Gratitude turns what we have into enough, and more. It turns denial into acceptance, chaos into order, confusion into clarity.

The root of joy is gratefulness. It is not joy that makes us grateful; it is gratitude that makes us joyful.

Gratitude is the wine for the soul. Go on. Get drunk.

When you arise in the morning, think of what a precious privilege it is to be alive - to breathe, to think, to enjoy, to love.

Gratitude is the ability to experience life as a gift. It liberates us from the prison of want.

Appreciation is a wonderful thing. It makes what is excellent in others belong to us as well.

Gratitude is the memory of the heart.

In ordinary life, we hardly realize that we receive a great deal more than we give, and that it is only with gratitude that life becomes rich.

Gratitude is not only the greatest of virtues, but the parent of all others.

When eating fruit, remember the one who planted the tree.

The struggle ends when gratitude begins.

Gratitude makes sense of our past, brings peace for today, and creates a vision for tomorrow.

Thankfulness is the beginning of gratitude. Gratitude is the completion of thankfulness.

Gratitude is the most exquisite form of courtesy.

We can complain because rose bushes have thorns, or rejoice because thorn bushes have roses.

Gratitude is when memory is stored in the heart and not in the mind.

Acknowledging the good that you already have in your life is the foundation for all abundance.

Gratitude is the fairest blossom which springs from the soul.

What separates privilege from entitlement is gratitude.

Gratitude is the sign of noble souls.

Silent gratitude isn't very much use to anyone.

Gratitude is the music of the heart, when its chords are swept by the breeze of kindness.

He is a wise man who does not grieve for the things which he has not, but rejoices for those which he has.

When we give cheerfully and accept gratefully, everyone is blessed.
