Happiness

The art of finding joy in the journey, not just the destination

Productive people are happier.

Happiness is outside of your comfort zone.

Action may not always bring happiness, but there is no happiness without action.

A fit body, a calm mind, a house full of love. These things cannot be bought - they must be earned.

Happiness is not something ready made. It comes from your own actions.

The secret of happiness is not in doing what one likes, but in liking what one does.

Happiness is when what you think, what you say, and what you do are in harmony.

Happiness is not a goal; it is a by-product of a life well lived.

The greatest happiness you can have is knowing that you do not necessarily require happiness.

Happiness is not the absence of problems, it's the ability to deal with them.

True happiness comes from the joy of deeds well done, the zest of creating things new.

Happiness is a direction, not a place.

The happiness of your life depends upon the quality of your thoughts.

Happiness is not in the mere possession of money; it lies in the joy of achievement.

Happiness is a choice that requires effort at times.

Happiness is not having what you want, but wanting what you have.

Happiness is a state of activity.

The foolish man seeks happiness in the distance; the wise grows it under his feet.

Happiness is not a station you arrive at, but a manner of traveling.

Happiness is a form of courage.

Happiness is not a possession to be prized, but a quality of thought, a state of mind.

Happiness is not a reward - it is a consequence.
