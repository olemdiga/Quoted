Goal Setting

The architecture of achievement - turning vision into reality

Know your goal before starting any task.

A goal properly set is halfway reached.

Setting goals is the first step in turning the invisible into the visible.

Goals are dreams with deadlines.

What you get by achieving your goals is not as important as what you become by achieving your goals.

The trouble with not having a goal is that you can spend your life running up and down the field and never score.

If you don't know where you are going, you'll end up someplace else.

Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment.

Set goals that make you want to jump out of bed in the morning.

A goal without a plan is just a wish.

Your goals are the road maps that guide you and show you what is possible for your life.

The greater danger for most of us lies not in setting our aim too high and falling short, but in setting our aim too low and achieving our mark.

Goals are the fuel in the furnace of achievement.

You should set goals beyond your reach so you always have something to live for.

If you want to be happy, set a goal that commands your thoughts, liberates your energy, and inspires your hopes.

All successful people have a goal. No one can get anywhere unless he knows where he wants to go and what he wants to be or do.

The discipline you learn and the character you build from setting and achieving goals can be applied to any situation in life.

Give yourself a deadline. A goal is a dream with a finish line.

When you set a goal, you create a new standard for yourself that raises your self-esteem with each achievement.

Goal setting is not only about choosing the rewards you want to enjoy, but also the costs you are willing to pay.

Write down your goals. The act of writing makes things real and increases commitment.

Break your big goals into small steps and take the first step right away.

Your goals should scare you a little and excite you a lot.

Review your goals daily. What you focus on expands.

Goals allow you to control the direction of change in your favor.

Setting goals is like drawing a map for your future self to follow.

The quality of your goals determines the quality of your life.

Goals transform a random walk into a chase.

When your goals are clear, you will come up with exactly the right answer at exactly the right time.
