Inflation

The silent thief that steals purchasing power while you sleep

Inflation is taxation without legislation.

Inflation is the one form of taxation that can be imposed without legislation.

Inflation is always and everywhere a monetary phenomenon.

Inflation is the hidden tax that erodes savings while governments pretend it's economic growth.

Inflation punishes savers and rewards debtors. It's a transfer of wealth from the patient to the reckless.

The most important thing about inflation is that it's a silent thief. You don't notice it day to day, but over decades it steals half your purchasing power.

Inflation is the government's way of defaulting on its debts without formally admitting it.

Money is a store of value. Inflation is the leak in that store.

Inflation is the reason cash is trash over the long term.

Inflation expectations become self-fulfilling: if everyone believes prices will rise, they raise prices, and prices rise.

Inflation is a disease, but a little bit of it is necessary to keep the economy from seizing up completely.

Inflation is the price we pay for the illusion that we can spend more than we produce indefinitely.

Inflation is not rising prices; it's the debasement of the currency that rising prices reflect.

Inflation redistributes wealth from those who hold money to those who hold assets.

Inflation is the canary in the coal mine of monetary policy.

Inflation is a regressive tax that hits the poor hardest because they hold more of their wealth in cash.

Inflation is the result of too much money chasing too few goods.

Inflation is the economic equivalent of background radiation: low levels are tolerable, high levels are catastrophic.

Inflation destroys the middle class by eroding the value of savings while inflating the cost of living.

Inflation is the reason a dollar saved is not a dollar earned - it's a dollar slowly dying.

Inflation is the friend of the asset owner and the enemy of the wage earner.

Inflation is the ultimate betrayal of the social contract between government and citizen.

Inflation is like toothpaste: once it's out of the tube, it's very hard to put back in.

Inflation is the reason retirement planning is so difficult: you're aiming at a target that keeps moving.

Inflation is the silent partner in every long-term financial decision. Ignore it at your peril.
