Enough

The antidote to more. The wealth of having exactly what you need.

The richest person is not the one who has the most, but the one who needs the least.

You chase more knowledge, more stimulation, more wins and never arrive. Enough is the destination you keep passing.

He who knows that enough is enough will always have enough.

The secret of happiness is not found in seeking more, but in developing the capacity to enjoy less.

Enough is not a quantity. It is a decision.

The appetite for more is infinite. The wisdom of enough is learned, not innate.

There are two ways to get enough. One is to accumulate more and more. The other is to desire less.

The man who has won millions at the cost of his conscience is a failure.

It is not the man who has too little, but the man who craves more, that is poor.

To have little is to possess. To have much is to be possessed.

Wealth consists not in having great possessions, but in having few wants.

Nothing is enough for the man to whom enough is too little.

If you are not satisfied with what you have, you will not be satisfied with what you get.

Enough is as good as a feast.

The ability to say "this is sufficient" is a superpower in a culture of endless wanting.

You will never have enough of what you don't really need.

The endless pursuit of more is the engine of dissatisfaction. Enough is the brake.

Enough is not settling. It is recognizing the point of diminishing returns and choosing to stop before the cost exceeds the value.

Knowing when to stop is the hardest skill. It requires more strength than continuing.

The word 'enough' is the most liberating word in the English language. It frees you from the tyranny of more.
