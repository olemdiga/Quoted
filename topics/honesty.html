Honesty

The courage to speak truth in a world of comfort

He who dares not offend cannot be honest.

Honesty is the first chapter in the book of wisdom.

No legacy is so rich as honesty.

Honesty is more than not lying. It is truth telling, truth speaking, truth living, and truth loving.

The high road is always respected. Honesty and integrity are always rewarded.

Honesty is the best policy - when there is money in it.

To be honest, as this world goes, is to be one man picked out of ten thousand.

Honesty is the fastest way to prevent a mistake from turning into a failure.

The truth is like a lion; you don't have to defend it. Let it loose; it will defend itself.

Honesty is the rarest wealth anyone can possess.

An honest man's the noblest work of God.

Honesty is the cornerstone of all success, without which confidence and ability to perform shall cease to exist.

Honesty is the single most important factor having a direct bearing on the final success of an individual.

Honesty is the best image.

Honesty is something you can't wear out.

Honesty is the highest form of intimacy.

Honesty is a very expensive gift. Don't expect it from cheap people.

Honesty is the ability to tell the truth even when it's difficult.
