Justice

The pursuit of fairness and moral rightness

If a law is unjust, a man is not only right to disobey it - he is obligated to do so.

Good people do not need laws to tell them to act responsibly, while bad people will find a way around the laws.

Justice will not be served until those who are unaffected are as outraged as those who are.

I guess the only time most people think about injustice is when it happens to them.

Mercy to the guilty is cruelty to the innocent.

Injustice anywhere is a threat to justice everywhere.

The arc of the moral universe is long, but it bends toward justice.

Justice delayed is justice denied.

True peace is not merely the absence of tension; it is the presence of justice.

Justice is what love looks like in public.

The dead cannot cry out for justice. It is a duty of the living to do so for them.

Justice without force is powerless; force without justice is tyrannical.

There may be times when we are powerless to prevent injustice, but there must never be a time when we fail to protest.

Justice is conscience, not a personal conscience but the conscience of the whole of humanity.

If you are neutral in situations of injustice, you have chosen the side of the oppressor.

The first duty of society is justice.

Justice is the constant and perpetual will to render to every man his due.

No nation is great that thinks justice too expensive to maintain.

Justice is truth in action.

The foundation of justice is good faith.

Justice is the ligament which holds civilized beings and civilized nations together.

The sword of justice has no scabbard.

Where there is no justice, there can be no secure peace.

Justice is the crowning glory of the virtues.
