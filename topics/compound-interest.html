Compound Interest

The eighth wonder of the world - where time multiplies effort

Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world. He who understands it, earns it; he who doesn't, pays it.

Compound interest is the most powerful force in the universe for those with time and discipline.

The first rule of compounding: never interrupt it unnecessarily.

Compounding works in every domain - money, knowledge, relationships, and habits all multiply with consistent investment.

The curve of compounding looks flat for years, then suddenly becomes vertical. Most people quit in the flat part.

Time is the essential ingredient in compounding. You can't rush it, but you can waste it.

Small, consistent actions compound into extraordinary results. The problem is we overestimate what we can do in a day and underestimate what we can do in a decade.

Compounding is the reward for patience in a world addicted to speed.

The difference between linear growth and exponential growth is the difference between arithmetic and geometric thinking.

Compounding works both ways. Small bad habits compound into disaster just as surely as small good habits compound into success.

The most important factor in compounding is not the rate of return, but the length of time you stay in the game.

Compounding is boring. That's why most people don't do it. The excitement comes later, but only for those who endured the boredom.

Knowledge compounds faster than money because it can't be taxed or inflated away.

The magic of compounding is that the gains from previous periods generate gains of their own. It's interest on interest on interest.

Compounding is the process by which small things become enormous through consistent repetition over time.

The best time to start compounding was twenty years ago. The second best time is today.

Compounding is the natural law of accumulation. It governs forests, fortunes, and fame alike.

The investor's worst enemy is not the market but the interruption of compounding through fear or impatience.

Compounding reveals the hidden architecture of time: small daily choices become the blueprint of your life.

The miracle of compounding is that it turns ordinary effort into extraordinary outcomes, given enough time.

Compounding is the universe's way of rewarding consistency and punishing impulsiveness.

The most powerful compounder is not money but character - it multiplies every other asset you have.

Compounding is silent in the beginning and deafening at the end.

The tragedy of not understanding compounding is that you don't know what you're missing until it's too late to start.

Compounding is the mathematical expression of the ancient wisdom that small, faithful efforts yield great harvests.
