Learning

The compound interest of knowledge

Lifelong learning compounds like interest - start early, never stop

It is harder to forget practice than it is to forget theory. Focus on practicing the learned skills.

Curiosity > credentials; chase questions, not degrees.

Teaching cements mastery; learn by sharing.

Diverse inputs spark breakthroughs; silo thinking stagnates.

Mistakes while learning are just part of the process.

Anything worth learning is worth a certain amount of effort.

The mind is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled.

Learning is the only thing the mind never exhausts, never fears, and never regrets.

The capacity to learn is a gift; the ability to learn is a skill; the willingness to learn is a choice.

Live as if you were to die tomorrow. Learn as if you were to live forever.

The beautiful thing about learning is that nobody can take it away from you.

Learning is not attained by chance, it must be sought for with ardor and diligence.

The expert in anything was once a beginner.

Education is the kindling of a flame, not the filling of a vessel.

Learning without reflection is a waste. Reflection without learning is dangerous.

The more that you read, the more things you will know. The more that you learn, the more places you'll go.

Change is the end result of all true learning.

Learning is not a spectator sport. You must be actively engaged.

The greatest enemy of learning is knowing.

Learning is like rowing upstream: not to advance is to drop back.

Wisdom is not a product of schooling but of the lifelong attempt to acquire it.

You don't learn to walk by following rules. You learn by doing, and by falling over.
