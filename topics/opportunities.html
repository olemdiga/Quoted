Opportunities

Recognizing and seizing the moments that change everything

Opportunities don't happen. You create them.

Luck is what happens when preparation meets opportunity.

The secret of success in life is to be ready for opportunity when it comes.

In the middle of difficulty lies opportunity.

Don't wait for extraordinary opportunities. Seize common occasions and make them great.

Opportunity is missed by most people because it is dressed in overalls and looks like work.

The biggest opportunities often look like problems at first glance.

When one door closes, another opens; but we often look so long at the closed door that we miss the one that opened.

Great opportunities come to all, but many do not know they have met them.

The wise man makes more opportunities than he finds.

Your big opportunity may be right where you are now.

Opportunities are usually disguised as hard work, so most people don't recognize them.

Small opportunities are often the beginning of great enterprises.

The best opportunities require you to bet on yourself when no one else will.

Opportunity dances with those already on the dance floor.

Work hard, apply yourself, and be ready. When the opportunity comes you'll be able to handle it.
