Discipline

The bridge between goals and accomplishment

We must suffer one of two pains, either the pain of discipline or the pain of regret and disappointment.

Discipline is choosing what you want most over what you want now.

Through discipline comes freedom.

Which do you want - the pain of staying where you are, or the pain of growth?

The obstacle is the way.

There is no coming to consciousness without pain.

Easy choices, hard life. Hard choices, easy life.

Go for the pain of discipline, it weighs ounces. The pain of regret weighs tons.

The distance between dreams and reality is called discipline.

It's not the lack of intelligence, it's the lack of focus that is stopping you.

Doing it right is hard. Doing it over is harder.

Discipline is the soul of an army. It makes small numbers formidable; procures success to the weak, and esteem to all.

The first and greatest victory is to conquer yourself; to be conquered by yourself is of all things most shameful and vile.

Discipline is remembering what you want.

Motivation gets you started. Discipline keeps you going.

Small disciplines repeated with consistency every day lead to great achievements gained slowly over time.

Discipline is the foundation upon which all success is built.

The disciplined mind leads to happiness, and the undisciplined mind leads to suffering.

Discipline is the refining fire by which talent becomes ability.

No stream or gas drives anything until it is confined. No life grows great until it is focused, dedicated, disciplined.

Discipline is the bridge between thought and accomplishment.
