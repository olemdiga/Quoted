Execution

A good plan violently executed now is better than a perfect plan next week

A good plan violently executed now is better than a perfect plan next week.

Do what you can, where you are, with what you have.

The most difficult is the decision to act, the rest is just tenacity.

The right idea not executed is worth nothing and a mediocre one well executed can be worth much.

Focus on transforming yourself into the kind of person who can bring ideas to life.

Ideas are welcomed, execution is worshipped.

The lazy don't know how to start. The losers don't know how to finish. The legends don't know how to stop.

Execution is everything. The best idea is worthless without it.

Vision without execution is hallucination.

The world rewards execution, not excuses.

Done is better than perfect.

Execution turns the invisible into the visible, the impossible into the possible.

Success is 10% inspiration and 90% perspiration - but 100% execution.

The gap between knowing and doing is far greater than the gap between ignorance and knowledge.

Execution is the great unaddressed issue in most organizations today.

Strategy gets you on the playing field, but execution wins the game.

Execution is the ability to mesh strategy with reality, align people with goals, and achieve promised results.

Great execution is at least 10 times more important and 100 times harder than a great idea.
