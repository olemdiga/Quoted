Improving

The art of becoming who you are capable of being

The fastest way to learn is to do things the slow way.

He who asks a question is a fool for five minutes. He who does not ask remains a fool forever.

You can only be as good as you dare to be bad.

It is impossible for a man to learn what he thinks he already knows.

Devote the rest of your life to making progress.

Continuous improvement is better than delayed perfection.

The only person you should try to be better than is the person you were yesterday.

Growth is painful. Change is painful. But nothing is as painful as staying stuck somewhere you don't belong.

Small daily improvements are the key to staggering long-term results.

If you are not embarrassed by the first version of your product, you've launched too late.

The expert in anything was once a beginner.

Progress is impossible without change, and those who cannot change their minds cannot change anything.

Don't be afraid to give up the good to go for the great.

Your life does not get better by chance, it gets better by change.

What you get by achieving your goals is not as important as what you become by achieving your goals.

If you want to improve, be content to be thought foolish and stupid.

The journey of self-improvement begins with self-awareness.

You cannot change your destination overnight, but you can change your direction overnight.

Improvement begins with I.

The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second best time is now.

Don't wish it were easier, wish you were better.

The only thing standing between you and your goal is the story you keep telling yourself.

The distance between your dreams and reality is called action.
