Paradoxes

The beautiful contradictions where logic meets mystery

The only true wisdom is in knowing you know nothing.

This sentence is false.

The more you learn, the less you know.

To remain the same, everything must change.

The only constant is change.

Less is more.

The faster you go, the slower you arrive.

You must lose yourself to find yourself.

The beginning of the end is the end of the beginning.

To see is to be blind; to be blind is to see.

The more choices you have, the less satisfied you are with any choice.

To control something, you must first let it go.

The only way to be truly free is to accept complete responsibility.

The more connected we become, the more isolated we feel.

To find peace, you must embrace conflict.

The truth that sets you free is the truth you'd rather not see.

The more you try to remember, the more you forget.

To be certain is to be wrong about the nature of reality.

The only thing impossible is impossibility itself.

The more security you seek, the less security you have.

To understand everything is to forgive everything.

The loudest silence speaks the most.

The straight path is often the most winding.

To live fully, you must accept death completely.

The more you give, the more you receive.

The simplest answers are the most complex to understand.

To be heard, sometimes you must be silent.

The whole is greater than the sum of its parts, yet contains nothing more than those parts.

The journey is the destination, yet without a destination there is no journey.

To love is to be vulnerable, yet vulnerability is the source of true strength.

The more you know, the more you realize how much you don't know.
