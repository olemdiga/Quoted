Focus & Concentration

The art of directing attention in a world designed to distract

Focusing is an art. Time itself is not your limitation: Attention is. Busy implies out of control. Lack of time is lack of priorities.

You don't need more time. You need fewer distractions.

Learn to be indifferent to what makes no difference.

Focusing is about saying no.

No one cares about you until you win. No one is ever ignoring you, everyone is focused on their goals, and you should be doing the same.

Concentration is the secret of strength in politics, in war, in trade, in short in all management of human affairs.

The ability to concentrate and to use your time well is everything if you want to succeed.

Focus is a matter of deciding what things you're not going to do.

What you stay focused on will grow.

The sun's energy warms the world. But when you focus it through a magnifying glass it can start a fire. Focus is so powerful.

Lack of direction, not lack of time, is the problem. We all have twenty-four hour days.

The more you concentrate, the more you can concentrate.

Concentrate all your thoughts upon the work at hand. The sun's rays do not burn until brought to a focus.

Your focus determines your reality.

The successful warrior is the average man, with laser-like focus.

Focusing is about intentional exclusion - the art of ignoring what doesn't matter.

Multitasking is the enemy of focus. It's merely the opportunity to screw up more than one thing at a time.

The difference between successful people and very successful people is that very successful people say 'no' to almost everything.

Concentration is the root of all the higher abilities in man.

The ability to focus attention on important things is a defining characteristic of intelligence.

Focus is not about saying yes to the right things. It's about saying no to the hundred other good ideas.

Deep focus is so rare today that it has become a superpower.

Your attention is the most valuable currency you possess - spend it wisely.

Concentration requires not just the absence of distraction, but the presence of engagement.

The focused mind can pierce through stone. Indecision, distraction - these are the enemies of progress.

In the age of distraction, the ability to focus is the new competitive advantage.

Mastering others is strength. Mastering yourself is true power. And mastery begins with focus.
