Commitment

The bridge between intention and achievement

Those who commit to nothing are distracted by everything.

Without commitment, you cannot have depth in anything, whether it's a relationship, a business or a hobby.

Commitment is an act, not a word.

Commitment is what transforms a promise into a reality.

Become uncomfortable but when life gets blurry, just adjust your focus. Random wandering will not move you forward. Pick one thing and stick with it to completion.

Half-hearted commitment is just delayed abandonment.

The quality of your commitments determines the quality of your life.

Commitment means staying loyal to what you said you were going to do long after the mood you said it in has left you.

There's a difference between interest and commitment. When you're interested in something, you do it only when it's convenient. When you're committed, you accept no excuses.

Commitment is choosing to burn your escape routes.

The moment of commitment is when you move from thinking about what you want to doing what it takes.

Commitment is the enemy of resistance, the destroyer of procrastination.

Shallow commitments create shallow results. Deep commitments create transformation.

Commitment is the courage to choose your constraints.

Until one is committed, there is hesitancy, the chance to draw back, always ineffectiveness.

Commitment is the foundation of all meaningful achievement.

The price of commitment is paid in advance, but the rewards are collected over a lifetime.

Commitment is what separates the professionals from the amateurs.

Your commitments are the architecture of your character.

Commitment is the decision to see something through regardless of how you feel in the moment.

The depth of your commitment determines the height of your achievement.
