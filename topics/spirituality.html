The goal of spirituality is not to escape life, but to live it more deeply.

True spirituality makes you more human, not less.

Enlightenment is not about becoming someone special, but about realizing you were never separate.

The most spiritual thing you can do is embrace your humanity.

Spirituality begins where religion ends - in the direct experience of mystery.

The divine is not somewhere else, but right here in this ordinary moment.

Spiritual maturity is measured by your capacity for uncertainty.

The most profound spiritual experiences often come through the most ordinary activities.

True spirituality is inclusive, not exclusive - it embraces all of life.

Your spiritual practice should serve your life, not the other way around.
